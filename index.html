About the Project

Many people are unaware of the government schemes available to them. Information can be spread across different websites, and understanding eligibility conditions may take time. This project proposes a simpler way to discover potentially relevant schemes.

🔎

The Problem

People may find it difficult to identify schemes relevant to their circumstances and understand the requirements.

🤖

Our Proposed Solution

An AI-supported system that could compare user-provided details with scheme eligibility criteria and suggest relevant options.

🔗

Reliable Next Steps

Help users reach official government scheme pages to verify details and learn how to apply.

Key Benefits

The project aims to make public scheme information easier to navigate.

Save time

Reduce the effort involved in searching multiple sources.

Clearer eligibility

Present important eligibility conditions in simple language.

Wider awareness

Help people discover schemes they may not know about.

How it could work

A proposed user journey for the future system.

01

Enter details

Provide relevant information such as age, location, income range or occupation.

02

Check criteria

The system compares details with available scheme requirements.

03

Review matches

See schemes that may be relevant and review their eligibility conditions.

04

Verify officially

Confirm current rules and apply only through official government channels.

Important: This page is an informational project concept, not an official government service. Eligibility and scheme rules can change. Always verify information on the relevant official government website. Do not share Aadhaar numbers, bank details or other sensitive personal information on this demo page.
